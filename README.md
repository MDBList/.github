# MDBList

**MDBList** is a movie and TV show discovery, tracking, and list management platform.

Build powerful lists, track what you watch, sync your media libraries, discover what to watch next, and connect MDBList with your favorite media platforms.

🌐 [mdblist.com](https://mdblist.com)

## Official Integrations

### Plex
Sync your Plex watch history, ratings, watchlist, and library with MDBList.

### Kodi
Two-way watch history and ratings sync, library/collection sync, and MDBList watchlist integration.

[GitHub](https://github.com/MDBList/kodi-mdblist-scrobbler)

### Jellyfin
Two-way watch history and ratings sync, library/collection sync, and live scrobbling.

[GitHub](https://github.com/MDBList/jellyfin-plugin-mdblist)

### Emby
Two-way watch history and ratings sync, library/collection sync, and live scrobbling.

[GitHub](https://github.com/MDBList/emby-plugin-mdblist)

## Reeel

**Reeel** is the official MDBList companion app for iOS and Android.

Track movies and shows, manage your watchlist, follow your Up Next calendar, discover new titles, and access your MDBList account on the go.

🌐 [reeel.app](https://reeel.app)
