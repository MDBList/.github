# MDBList

**MDBList** is a movie and TV show discovery, tracking, and list management platform.

Create powerful lists, track what you watch, and sync your media libraries across your favorite platforms.

🌐 [mdblist.com](https://mdblist.com)

## Integrations

### [Kodi](https://github.com/MDBList/kodi-mdblist-scrobbler)
Two-way watch history and ratings sync, library sync, and watchlist integration.

### [Jellyfin](https://github.com/MDBList/jellyfin-plugin-mdblist)
Two-way watch history and ratings sync, library sync, and live scrobbling.

### [Emby](https://github.com/MDBList/emby-plugin-mdblist)
Two-way watch history and ratings sync, library sync, and live scrobbling.

## [Reeel](https://reeel.app)

Official MDBList companion app for iOS and Android.
